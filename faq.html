FAQ

Everything you need to know about our website and educational games.

Are the games safe for my child?

Yes. All of our content is designed to be child-friendly and safe. We do not have chat features or external links within our games.

What age group are the games for?

Our games are primarily designed for children aged 3-8, focusing on early learning concepts like numbers, letters, colors, and shapes.

Do I need to install anything?

No! All our games run directly in your web browser on any device, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones. No downloads needed.

Are the games free?

Yes, all of our educational games are completely free to play.

How are game scores saved?

High scores for our games are saved locally in your browser's storage. They are not shared publicly.

Can I reset the scores?

Yes, you can clear your browser's cache and site data for our website to reset all saved scores.

Do the games work on mobile?

Absolutely! Our website and all our games are fully responsive and designed to work great on mobile phones and tablets.

Do the games have sound?

Yes, our games include sounds and music to make the experience more engaging. You can typically mute sound within the game or by muting your device.

How do you handle my child's data?

We take privacy very seriously. We do not collect any personally identifiable information from children. Please read our Privacy Policy for more details.

How can I suggest an idea for a new game?

We'd love to hear from you! Please use our contact form to send us your ideas and feedback.
