Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-22

Welcome to Kids Learning World. Your child's privacy is a top priority for us. We are committed to creating a safe and secure online learning environment. This policy is written in plain language to help you, as a parent or guardian, understand our practices. Our goal is to collect the minimum data necessary to provide and improve our educational games.

1. Information We Collect

We design our services to collect minimal information from our users. Here’s what we collect and why:

  • Gameplay Data (Locally Stored): We may store game data, such as high scores or progress, directly in the browser on your device using `localStorage`. This information is not sent to our servers and is not personally identifiable.
  • Contact Information (from Parents/Guardians): If you, as a parent or guardian, contact us directly via our contact form or email, we will have a record of our correspondence, including your name and email address, to provide you with support. We do not solicit this information from children.
  • Anonymous Technical & Usage Data: We collect anonymous, aggregated data about how users interact with our website, such as browser type, device type, and pages visited. This information does not identify individual users and helps us understand traffic, improve site performance, and ensure compatibility.

2. How We Use Your Information

Our primary goal is to provide a fun and safe learning environment. We use the limited information we collect to:

  • Operate and maintain our educational games and website.
  • Display local leaderboards and personal high scores on your device only.
  • Communicate with parents or guardians for support or to respond to feedback.
  • Monitor and analyze anonymous usage data to improve our services and develop new games.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service to ensure a safe environment for all users.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent any personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to a valid legal request.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party companies for services like website hosting. These providers do not have access to any personal information we may hold from parent communications.

4. Your Rights and Choices (for Parents and Guardians)

As a parent or guardian, you have control over your child's information. You have the right to:

  • Review and Delete Information: You can contact us to review any personal information we may have collected from you and request its deletion.
  • Clear Local Data: You can clear all locally stored game data, such as high scores, by clearing your browser's cache and site data for our website.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information we hold. Since we store most game data locally on your device, the security of that data is primarily managed by the security of your device and browser.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are designed for children. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information (like full name, address, or phone number) from children. Our games do not include features like chat rooms or public profiles that would allow children to share personal information.

7. Contact Us

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please don't hesitate to contact us at privacy@kidslearningworld.com. We're here to help.
